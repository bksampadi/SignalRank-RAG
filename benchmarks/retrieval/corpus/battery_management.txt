Battery-management systems track voltage, current, temperature, state of charge, and other operating conditions in rechargeable lithium-ion cells. During charging and discharging, lithium ions move between electrodes, while control electronics attempt to keep the cell within safe limits. High temperatures, excessive current, deep cycling, and repeated rapid charging can accelerate degradation. The management system can reduce these stresses by limiting charging rates or adjusting operation, but it does not itself store the electrical energy.
