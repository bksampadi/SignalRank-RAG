Regional climate monitoring combines long-term records from weather stations, ocean buoys, satellites, and other observing systems. These measurements can include temperature, precipitation, wind, pressure, humidity, and sea-surface conditions. Climate datasets are used to identify persistent patterns and changes over years or decades rather than to describe only the immediate state of the atmosphere at one location. Forecast models may use some of the same kinds of observations, but climate analysis focuses on long-term behaviour rather than short-term local measurement.
