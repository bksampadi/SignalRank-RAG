Weather stations monitor local atmospheric conditions using instruments that record temperature, rainfall, wind speed, wind direction, humidity, and barometric pressure. A rain gauge measures precipitation, a thermometer records air temperature, and an anemometer measures wind speed. These observations are usually collected repeatedly so that changes over hours or days can be compared.

Forecasting is not the same as measurement. Forecasts combine recent observations with numerical models of the atmosphere to estimate future conditions. A station can therefore provide direct evidence about what is happening at a location now, while a forecast predicts what may happen later.
