Dinosaurs were a diverse group of reptiles that dominated many terrestrial ecosystems during the Mesozoic Era. Fossils preserve evidence about their anatomy, growth, diet, movement, and evolutionary relationships. Some species were enormous herbivores, while others were agile predators or small feathered animals.

Non-avian dinosaurs disappeared during the mass extinction at the end of the Cretaceous Period about 66 million years ago. Evidence strongly links this event to the impact of a large asteroid near what is now the Yucatán Peninsula, although volcanic activity and long-term environmental changes may also have influenced ecosystems. Modern birds are the surviving avian lineage of dinosaurs.
