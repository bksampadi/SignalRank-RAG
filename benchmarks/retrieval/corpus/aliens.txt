Scientists searching for extraterrestrial life do not assume that intelligent aliens have already been discovered. Instead, astrobiology investigates environments where life might be possible, while searches for technosignatures look for observable evidence that could be produced by advanced technology.

Researchers may examine planetary atmospheres for unusual chemical combinations, listen for narrow-band radio signals, or search astronomical data for other anomalies. Any candidate signal must be tested against natural explanations, instrumental effects, satellites, and human interference before it could be considered evidence of extraterrestrial technology.
